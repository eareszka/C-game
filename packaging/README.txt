FOUR CASTLE CHRONICLES - beta
=============================

Thanks for testing! This is an early beta. Saves may be wiped between
versions: a save from an older build may not continue (the title says
so), and NEW GAME replaces it.

RUNNING
  Unzip the whole folder anywhere and double-click game.exe.
  Keep game.exe next to its .dll files and the assets folder.
  Your save is save.dat, in this same folder. settings.txt beside it
  remembers the volumes and fullscreen (OPTIONS on the title screen).

CONTROLS
  Arrows / WASD     move
  Shift             sprint
  Ctrl              crouch
  Z / Enter / Space confirm, talk, open; shoot in battle (hold)
  X                 back
  Q / E             switch weapon
  Tab               menu (items, crafting, save)
  M                 map
  Mouse wheel       zoom
  F11               fullscreen
  Hold Esc 3 s      quit

TIPS
  Talk to people in Bloom, the town by your house.
  The book shop sells recipe books; craft a raft to leave the island.
  If you lose a fight you wake up at home, with nothing lost.
  Nothing grows back: trees you cut and enemies you beat are gone for good.
  The raft is worn like boots; take it off in ITEMS if you need to.

REPORTING BUGS
  Please leave a comment on the itch.io page: what happened, what you
  expected, and what you were doing just before. A screenshot helps.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES IN THIS BETA
  - No music yet. The sound effects are synthesized and will be replaced.
  - Some enemies (84-88) still have rough sprites, and many show their
    battle sprite shrunk down on the map.
  - The beta ends after the first three bosses; other boss sites say
    "NOT YET".
  - Castles, the third town and some house interiors are not built yet.
