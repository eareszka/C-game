Long menacing idle, facing left. The whole creature moves: the huge grey and purple serpent's neck rises and sinks, its head sways, blood drips from its mouth, its one tail curls and uncurls and stays one solid tapering tail, its coil swells slowly. The small hooded creature in the canoe moves too: it rocks, looks around and works the paddle back and forth, and the canoe rocks gently in the coil. Camera fixed, same size, same design, no water.
