# Job: 91 Kurrea

- **Tier:** Severe
- **Size:** 77 px on its longer side. Within 4 px either way is fine.
- **Colours:** at most 8 plus black.
- **Look:** Kurrea: a dark violet snake lying in one big round coil on the ground, its neck rising at the right into a raised wedge-shaped head facing right with a small yellow eye. A plain white belly stripe runs along the underside of the neck and coil. Four crystals of different sizes and colours grow out of the back of the coil at the left: a large pink pair, a tall mint one, a sky blue one and a small gold one.
- **Draw:** its first idle pose, in the same arrangement, proportions and size as the left image of reference.png.
- **Note:** reference.png, on grey, shows two sprites. The left one is the new Kurrea design: follow its shapes, arrangement and colours as closely as the palette allows, but not its soft edges: draw it crisply as RULE.md says. The right one is 93 Chipique, a sprite the user approved: Kurrea is a different creature of the same snake type, so match 93's drawing: the same wedge head, thick black outline, two flat tones for the body and one plain white belly stripe. Colours: body 664b95 with light b69cee as on 93 but darker overall is fine, belly fcfcfc, eye a small solid yellow mark.
- **Must have:** The user: "93 and 91 should look like similar snake types despite being completly different creatures".
- **Must have:** The old Kurrea was rejected for an even ring of rainbow spikes and stamped plates down the body. No scales, plates, ticks or segment lines anywhere, the belly stripe included: the reference has faint ticks on the belly that must not be copied.
- **Must have:** The four crystals differ from each other in size, angle and colour. Each crystal is its own closed shape with at most two tones of its colour.
- **Must have:** The coil must read on the black arena: the dark violet stays clearly lighter than black.
