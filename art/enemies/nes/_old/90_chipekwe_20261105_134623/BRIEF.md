It has a real rhino head: a broad wedge-shaped face narrowing down to a wide snout, pointed ears angled up and out, one big pale horn rising straight up from the snout between the eyes, and a short curved tusk on each side of the snout. Small orange eyes sit either side of the horn. A ridged hump shows above the head. Two massive front legs stand wide apart on big pale-clawed feet, with the two hind legs in shadow between them under a dark belly.
