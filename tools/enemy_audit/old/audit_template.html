The art style, as it stands

The game has a real shared style at the level of materials, but not yet a shared way of drawing at large sizes.

  • What all 101 share: every colour is from the game's palette, with no half-transparent pixels, and no sprite goes over 8 colours plus black. This is why even the weak ones don't look like they come from another game at a glance.
  • Where it works: up to about 50 px. Sprites are chunky and quick to read, with each pixel doing a job, like your picks.
  • Where it drifts: from about 70 px up, three different ways of drawing appear. Some are built from closed parts like Anaye, some cover a shape in repeated texture like 98 Boiuna, and some use soft shading like 49 Physeter.
  • NES style, loosely: this is NES-flavoured, not what the hardware could do. A real NES sprite had 3 colours plus transparent per small tile, and a 282 px enemy would nearly fill its screen. That's fine for this game, but it means the look is set by your picks, not by NES limits.

Why it breaks down

  • Big sprites have no target. Only {{BIG_BENCH_N}} of the {{BENCH_N}} standard sprites are over 66 px: {{BIG_BENCH}}. Nothing over {{BENCH_MAX}} px has been approved, and {{OVER_BENCH}} enemies are bigger than that, so their agents had to guess how a big sprite should look.
  • Most were drawn under an old rule. {{FIRST_RULE}} of the {{DRAWN_N}} redraws came from the first rule, which pushed agents to fill big bodies with texture. {{LOOPS}} of those {{DRAWN_N}} were drawn with loops in code, which is how identical bricks and scales get made. Only 90 used the closed-parts rule.
  • Your two approvals are the plainest in their tier. 90 has {{D90}} colour patches per 100 px and 93 has {{D93}}, against a middle of {{SEV_MED}} for the rest of Severe. They also differ from each other: 90 is {{I90}}% inner black and 93 is {{I93}}%. So Severe currently holds three levels of detail.
  • The rule is stricter than your own picks. It asks for two tones per material and 4 to 5 colours. {{TONES_OVER}} of the {{BENCH_N}} standard sprites go over the tone limit and {{COLOURS_OVER}} go over 5 colours, and {{CAST_OVER}} of all 101 break at least one of the two. Either the numbers or the picks need to give.
  • Size order is out of step in the bosses. After your swaps, Boss 2's 34 Anaye ({{B34}} px) and 44 Itqiirpak ({{B44}} px) are much smaller than Boss 1's 23 Olgoi-Khorkhoi ({{B23}} px) and 30 Dajna ({{B30}} px).

What's next

  1. Set a target for big sprites. Your call. I'd redraw 49 Physeter under the closed-parts rule first. Its shape is simple, so it's a cheap test, and if you approve it, the other bosses have something to follow.
  2. Work through the {{N_NO}} that don't match, as you name them. Then the {{N_CLOSE}} close ones, where touch-ups may do.
  3. Settle the rule's limits. Your call. I'd raise it to 3 tones and up to 8 colours, since that's what your picks use, and keep the rest.
  4. Decide whether the boss sizes stay. Boss 2 is currently smaller than Boss 1 at the top end.
  5. Build the poses and install step. It doesn't exist yet, so the game still shows the old sprites for all {{DRAWN_N}} redraws, including 90 and 93. Only your {{PICKS_N}} kept picks are what players see today.
